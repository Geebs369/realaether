GMRS / VHF note

GMRS, 2 meters, and 70 cm are mostly line-of-sight and are not driven by the 10.7 cm flux. A strong geomagnetic storm can still add noise, and rare aurora can briefly enhance VHF. Use the Kp card as a “storm tonight” heads-up for nets.
